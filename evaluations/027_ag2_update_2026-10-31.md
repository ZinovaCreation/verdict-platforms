## Executive Summary

Ten GitHub security advisories were published for ag2ai/ag2 between 2026-09-23 and 2026-10-03, one of which (GHSA-42mf-vpmr-gw5r, command-filter bypass in `LocalShellEnvironment`) carries a CVSS 3.1 score of 9.9; the prior record stated zero advisories and zero CVEs. Two CVE identifiers are attributable to the `ag2` package in the trailing 12 months: CVE-2026-113663 (CVSS 3.1 8.8, newline injection in `SandboxShellTool`, assigned 2026-10-21 to GHSA-2h4w-29cv-2cmv, fixed in 1.1.1; the CVE List record remains RESERVED as of 2026-11-01) and CVE-2026-108543 (CVSS 3.1 6.3, CNA VulDB, path traversal in the classic `UserProxyAgent` code path affecting ag2 ≤ 0.13.4, published 2026-10-11, no fixed version recorded). The Layer 0 score moves from 40/85 to 45/85 (Tier C → Tier B): R falls 14 → 9 on the CVE count, maximum severity, patch status of CVE-2026-108543 and a recurring command-filter root cause; T rises 2 → 7 on the security policy added 2026-04-07, the advisory and CVE publication practice, and the vendor's published Data Security Policy; I rises 3 → 6 and C 4 → 6 on the current documentation of cancellation, human-in-the-loop approval, delegation scope and the configurable sandbox backends. V falls 12 → 11 (open core under Criterion-VSource-001; no API reference) and D rises 5 → 6 (no telemetry endpoint in the library; hosted-service policy states training on anonymized data). No CISA KEV entry exists for any AG2 CVE (KEV feed catalogVersion 2026.10.30, checked 2026-11-01).

## Contextual Analysis

The record changes because the window changed, not because the earlier capture was wrong about its own window. Every AG2 advisory and CVE on the public record was first disclosed between 2026-09-23 and 2026-10-11, after the 2026-03-31 capture. The ten advisories arrived in four batches aligned with releases 0.13.x, 1.1.0, 1.1.1 and 1.1.2, and in each batch the fix preceded or accompanied the advisory. That pattern — disclosure at fix time, with vectors, CWEs, affected ranges and reporter credits — is what the T dimension now scores, and it did not exist in the prior record because the security policy itself dates from 2026-04-07.

The R dimension records two things the Scorecard cannot show on its own. First, the most severe finding in the window, the 9.9 `LocalShellEnvironment` bypass, has no CVE identifier and therefore does not enter `max_cvss_12mo`; the framework records CVE-less advisories in the Incident Timeline and in this section, and readers assessing the shell tooling should read GHSA-42mf-vpmr-gw5r and GHSA-2h4w-29cv-2cmv together. The one counted high-severity CVE, CVE-2026-113663, is itself in an intermediate state: the ID is public on the advisory and was confirmed by the maintainer, but the CVE List record is RESERVED and no NVD record exists as of 2026-11-01, so readers searching NVD alone will not find it. KEV status was checked against the KEV feed of 2026-10-30 (1,766 entries): no AG2 entry. Second, CVE-2026-108543 was assigned by a third-party CNA (VulDB) from a public exploit report and lists no fixed version; the affected `UserProxyAgent` code path is part of the classic line, which AG2 moved out of the `ag2` package at 1.0.0 (2026-07-27) and continues to publish as the `autogen` package "in maintenance mode", receiving "security and critical bug fixes only" per its PyPI description. As of the update date the vendor has published no advisory, statement or `autogen` release for it; the CNA record states that early notification received no response. The engine records the elapsed time (20 days) and does not attribute a reason.

The recurrence finding rests on the vendor's own documents: the v1.1.2 release note describes "shell command-filter bypasses" in the plural across `SandboxShellTool` and `ShellAdapter` four days after 1.1.1 had addressed newline injection in the same filter, and GHSA-2h3q-9p6m-4hm4 itself links issue #3255 as the same design weakness through a different vector. The current documentation describes the reworked design (restricted mode executes an argv without a shell; approvals are bound to tool implementation rather than name; sub-task approvals are not inherited), which is the mitigating fact on the other side of the ledger.
